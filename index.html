Frequently asked questions

Is Velmora QR Studio free?

Yes. You can create and download QR codes without creating an account.

Can I make a Wi-Fi QR code?

Yes. Choose Wi-Fi connection, enter the network details, and generate a code that compatible devices can scan.

Do QR codes expire?

These are static QR codes. The encoded content does not expire, but a website link may stop working if its destination changes or becomes unavailable.
